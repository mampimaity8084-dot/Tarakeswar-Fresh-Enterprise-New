# V4.3 Rollback Guide

The V4.2 gold backup remains the rollback authority. Do not alter it.

For the isolated candidate, stop the candidate process and restore from `V4.2-IMPORTED/`. Do not delete or overwrite the backup. For a future approved rollout, retain compatibility endpoints, disable V4.3 browser routing, stop new V4.3 sessions, and reconcile pending payments before any cleanup.
